Clinical Safety Architecture & Anti-Alert Fatigue Engine

Designed in accordance with Indian Pharmacopoeia, Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO), and clinical pharmacology guidelines.

1

Brand De-obfuscation Engine

Deconstructs Indian commercial trade names (Combiflam, Zerodol-SP, Pan-D, Augmentin Duo) into discrete active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and standardizes dosages.

2

Organ & Demographics Guard

Calculates patient-specific maximum ceilings: weight-based pediatrics (mg/kg/dose), geriatric renal/hepatic safety margins, and eGFR CKD staging contraindications.

3

Anti-Alert Fatigue Triage

Explicitly addresses the problem statement's warning on alert fatigue: filters low-yield noise, categorizing findings into Critical (immediate harm), Moderate (timing/adjust), and Minor (collapsible).
